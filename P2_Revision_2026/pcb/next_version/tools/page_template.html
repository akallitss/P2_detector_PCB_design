Next PCB version: alignment crosses and the mesh-contact pad

Two changes we would like in the next version of the P2 basket PCB and its insulation masks, together with the space each one has. Everything here was measured on the current fabrication Gerbers, the V3B_final masks and the drift-frame drawing, and checked against the B2 drift-contact proposal.

1 · Bigger alignment crosses

Today. There are four crosses, 3 × 3 mm with 0.12 mm lines, each drawn identically on F.Cu and B.Cu. Both masks (M1 and M2) carry a rounded cross outline at the same four positions, 2.9 mm across with 1.16 mm wide arms, inside a resist square: 10 × 10 mm on the outer arc, about 6 × 6 mm on the inner arc. Lining up a 0.12 mm cross by eye is hard.

What limits the size. On copper, the GND hatch can always be re-poured around a bigger cross, so it isn't a limit. The real limits are pads, traces, holes and the board edge. In the masks, the resist square must not cover any connector pad.

The inner crosses sit under the drift-frame footprint, between the board-edge slot and the frame wall. That's fine for aligning the masks, which happens before the frame is mounted.

Proposal: the same mark at all four positions.
  • Copper (F.Cu and B.Cu): a cross 9 mm across (4.5 mm arms) with 0.3 mm lines. That is three times longer and 2.5 times thicker than today.
  • Masks (M1 and M2): a cross outline 10 mm across with 1.3 mm wide arms (0.5 mm tolerance each side of the copper line), in a 12.5 × 12.5 mm resist square.
  • Outer crosses 3 and 4 stay where they are.
  • Inner crosses 1 and 2 move 4 mm radially outward, away from the board-edge slot: to {{p1}} and {{p2}}. There they get the same 9 mm cross. The arms then run a little further under the frame, where the PCB already carries GND hatch.
  • If the inner crosses can't move, an asymmetric cross works too: short arms (≤ 1.3 mm) towards the slot, long arms away from it.

2 · A bigger mesh-contact pad

Today (bottom corner):

  • Gold pad: 2.93 × 2.81 mm, from (595.77, 1.29) to (598.70, 4.10).
  • Contact area: the mesh actually touches the pad only through the M2 window, which is 2.0 × 2.0 mm.
  • Where the mesh reaches: it is trimmed at the outer edge of the resist border, about 1.9 mm from the frame wall, so it never reaches the part of the pad nearest the wall.
  • V3B_final: it kept the window at its V1 size. So the masks haven't been enlarged yet.

Is the B2 drift pad in the way? Only from above. The mesh pad is bare mesh potential and the drift pad is bare drift potential, so they need 3 mm between them. The pad also has to stay outside the B2 mesh cut and resist island, so that the mesh still lies on it.

The nearest other front copper is the GND hatch beyond the frame wall, 1.25 mm away. That is unchanged from today, because none of the options grow towards the wall.

Proposal: option A. A 5.0 × 3.0 mm gold pad, with the M2 (and M1) window enlarged to about 4.1 × 2.2 mm. That roughly doubles the area the mesh can reach, and more than doubles the window. It stays clear of everything B2 adds.
Without B2, the readout pad /Sector0/Sig1 sits 0.9 mm from the mesh pad, so the pad can only grow towards the frame wall, where the mesh doesn't reach. A bigger mesh contact therefore comes almost for free with B2, and is hard without it. At the top corner the mesh pad is already 3.9 × 3.9 mm, but it is 1.6 mm from readout pad /Sector9/Sig128 and 1.3 mm from the active edge, so it has no room to grow there.

To decide with the PCB designers

  1. Cross line width (0.3 mm proposed) and whether the inner crosses can move 4 mm.
  2. Keep the crosses on both F.Cu and B.Cu, as today, or only on the side used for mask alignment?
  3. Mesh pad option A (5.0 × 3.0) or B (3.7 × 3.7), and the matching M1/M2 windows. We can generate the masks once this is decided.
  4. Whether the M trace keeps leaving from the pad's upper-right corner, as today.
