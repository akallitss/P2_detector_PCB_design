Next PCB version: alignment crosses, the corner-A drift contact and the screw holes

Three changes for the next version of the P2 basket PCB and its insulation masks. Everything here was measured on the current fabrication Gerbers, the V3B_final masks and the drift-frame drawing. The other revision items (connectors on the back, outline) are in the figures and tables of P2_Revision_2026/pcb/next_version/.

1 · Bigger alignment crosses

Today. There are four crosses, 3 × 3 mm with 0.12 mm lines, each drawn identically on F.Cu and B.Cu. Both masks (M1 and M2) carry a rounded cross outline at the same four positions, 2.9 mm across with 1.16 mm wide arms, inside a resist square: 10 × 10 mm on the outer arc, about 6 × 6 mm on the inner arc. Lining up a 0.12 mm cross by eye is hard.

What limits the size. On copper, the GND hatch can always be re-poured around a bigger cross, so it isn't a limit. The real limits are pads, traces, holes and the board edge. In the masks, the resist square must not cover any connector pad.

The inner crosses sit under the drift-frame footprint, between the board-edge slot and the frame wall. That's fine for aligning the masks, which happens before the frame is mounted.

Proposal: the same mark at all four positions.
  • Copper (F.Cu and B.Cu): a cross 9 mm across (4.5 mm arms) with 0.3 mm lines. That is three times longer and 2.5 times thicker than today.
  • Masks (M1 and M2): a cross outline 10 mm across with 1.3 mm wide arms (0.5 mm tolerance each side of the copper line), in a 12.5 × 12.5 mm resist square.
  • Outer crosses 3 and 4 stay where they are.
  • Inner crosses 1 and 2 move to r = 104.25 mm, at 47.5° and 12.5° (a mirror pair about the 30° axis): {{p1}} and {{p2}}. There they are clear of the board-edge slot and of the new screw holes, with 7.1 mm free round the centre. (An earlier proposal, 4 mm radially outward, ended up 3.7–4.2 mm from the new screw holes and is dropped.)
  • The 12.5 mm resist squares of crosses 1 and 2 reach r ≈ 113, 3 mm past the frame's inner wall (r 110) but before the pillar field (r ≈ 115). If that is a problem, a 10 mm square fits too.

2 · Corner A: the drift contact

Why it changes. The drift (window) HV contact moves onto the PCB, through a conductive-foam column pressed by the carbon drift piece onto a gold pad. The whole bottom-right corner of the mesh is cleared for it (no mesh for x ≥ 585.5 and y ≤ 15), so:

  • the readout pad /Sector0/Sig1 in that corner is given up;
  • the mesh-contact pad of this corner would get no mesh any more. The mesh is contacted at the top corner (B) only;
  • this corner's mesh line and its two M solder pads are free, and they now carry the drift.
Proposal (decided 26 Oct).
  • Drift pad: 3 × 3 mm, gold, at the centre of the cleared corner, {{pad}}.
  • Feed, all on F.Cu: a {{trace_w}} mm segment from the pad to the bend of today's mesh line at {{bend}}, then today's line as it is, under the frame, to the two M solder pads.
  • HV pads: the two M and the two D solder pads merge into one bigger pad, labelled D (8.4 × 6.9 mm, the envelope of the four), for the drift cable. The top GND pad is removed; the bottom GND pad stays, 3.26 mm from the D pad.
  • Removed: Sig1 (pad, via and back-copper trace; its connector pin is left floating); the mesh pad and the line's first stub (pad → bend); the top GND pad.
  • No via, no back copper. The first B2 patch put the pad at {{pad_old}} with a via and a back-copper route. That is not used any more.
  • Frame and carbon: the frame fills this corner at its lower level, with a slot for the foam centred on the pad, and the carbon flap rests on it.

3 · Screw holes

All screw holes become Ø3.5, at the same positions in the PCB, the drift frame and the carbon back (the numbers below are the same in all three).

  • Outer arc: today's holes stay where they are. They sit between the signal lines on the PCB, so they cannot move, and no holes are added on that arc. That is 13 holes, including the two outer corners.
  • The two inner corners stay as well.
  • Straight sides and inner arc: today's holes go, and new ones come at an equal pitch between the corner holes: bottom side 46.5 mm, top side 46.0 mm, inner arc 38.7 mm. The two straight sides differ in length by 5 mm (the top corner hole sits 5 mm in, clear of the gas inlet), so they are not exact mirror images. The two inner-arc holes are a mirror pair about the 30° axis.
Hole 13 (610.30, 14.50), kept on the outer arc, is plated today. Its pad is 2.65 mm from the corner-A drift line, under the 3 mm we aim for. Without plating (no pad) it is 3.2 mm from the hole edge.

To decide with the PCB designers

  1. Cross line width (0.3 mm proposed); keep the crosses on both F.Cu and B.Cu, as today, or only on the side used for mask alignment?
  2. Resist squares of crosses 1 and 2: 12.5 mm (reaching r ≈ 113) or 10 mm.
  3. The GND-pour keep-out round the drift net (3 mm proposed), and the HV-pad spacing for the drift voltage, and screw hole 13 (610.30, 14.50): kept on the outer arc, its plated pad is 2.65 mm from the drift line (non-plated: 3.2 mm).
  4. Plating of the 22 new screw holes (the 15 kept ones stay as today).
